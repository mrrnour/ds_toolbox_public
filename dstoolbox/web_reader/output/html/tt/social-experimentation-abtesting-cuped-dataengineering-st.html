One of the biggest unlocks in Experimentation Reporting? Stopping bare t-tests. Here's the insight that changed everything: A two-sample t-test is mathematically identical to OLS regression with a binary treatment indicator. Same p-value. Same standard error. The difference is what OLS lets you do NEXT. Add a single covariate : like a user's revenue from couple of weeks before the experiment and your variance shrinks by 30–60%. That's not a rounding error. That's half your sample size back. Narrower CIs. Higher power. Decisions that used to take 4 weeks now take 10 days. We didn't need more traffic. We needed smarter statistics. #Experimentation #ABTesting #CUPED #DataEngineering #Statistics
